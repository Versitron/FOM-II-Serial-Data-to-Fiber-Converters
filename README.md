## Why Choose FOM II Serial Data to Fiber Converters?

The FOM II Serial Data to Fiber Converters convert traditional serial data signals to optical signals, significantly increasing the communication range and providing transparent fiber optic data transmission. Whether it's a RS-232 to Fiber Converter or RS-530 to Fiber Converter, these units maintain the integrity of the original signal while providing electrical isolation and immunity to EMI/RFI interference.

These circuit card fiber optic modems are available in RS-232 Circuit Card Modem and RS-530 Circuit Card Modem configurations, making them highly compatible with legacy and modern data equipment. Additionally, these modems are DCE/DTE switch selectable, offering installation flexibility across various serial protocols.

- Supports Synchronous and Asynchronous Data Transmission  
  Whether your system transmits synchronous data over fiber optics or asynchronous data, these modems are optimized for both.

- Multiplexing Capabilities  
  Each device functions as a multiplexing fiber optic modem, supporting up to 8 channels (4 in each direction), ideal for applications requiring multiple signal paths.

- High Data Rates and Long-Distance Capabilities  
  With support for up to 5 Mbps fiber optic modem performance in the RS-530 model and 128Kbps fiber optic modem speeds in the RS-232 version, these modems are suitable for a wide range of applications. - Advanced Clock and Timing Options

- Rugged Design for Industrial Use  
  Available in both standalone serial fiber modem and rack mount fiber modem chassis configurations, the modems are ideal for industrial serial to fiber converters where reliability and durability are non-negotiable.
